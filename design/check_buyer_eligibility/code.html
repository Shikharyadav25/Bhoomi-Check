UP Revenue Code 2006, Section 89

"Restriction on transfer by a bhumidhar with transferable rights" — No person shall acquire land where total holding exceeds twelve and a half acres.

Tehsil Gazetted Standard
Official Statutory Digest: Section 89

Sub-section (1): No bhumidhar with transferable rights shall have the right to transfer any land by sale or gift where the transferee shall, as a result of the transfer, become entitled to land which together with land already held by him will in aggregate exceed twelve and a half acres (12.50 acres) in Uttar Pradesh.

Sub-section (2): Any transfer made in contravention of sub-section (1) shall be void, and the excess land shall vest in the State Government free from all encumbrances under Section 104.
